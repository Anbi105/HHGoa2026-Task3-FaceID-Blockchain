# Limitations

- Recognition quality depends on images, pose, lighting, and model limitations.
- Calibration is only representative of its small consenting dataset.
- Channel A searches only the indexed consenting public corpus.
- Reverse-search results can be unavailable, incomplete, noisy, or third-party controlled.
- When enabled, Channel B discloses a cropped face to third-party search infrastructure.
- A blockchain root proves integrity of a submitted evidence bundle, not that an identity claim is objectively true.
- This system is designed exclusively for consenting verification subjects, not arbitrary-person identification.
- On-chain bytes are immutable. Deleting local salt/evidence severs reproducibility but cannot erase chain data.
